# Company Brain: 10-minute demo script (full profile)

Before the demo: close Chrome and other large apps, then double-click **Start AI**. Wait until the chat window opens and sign in. All clients in the demo are fictional.

## 1. What it is (1 min)

Company Brain runs only on this Mac. The firewall blocks it from the internet, and it answers only from your own documents, with a source for every fact.

## 2. Ask a question (2 min)

1. Choose **Company Brain** and start a new chat.
2. When it asks "Which client?", type "Pellmore Dental Group".
3. Ask: "What fee did we agree with Pellmore Dental Group?"
4. Point out the source number, click source [1] and show the passage.
5. Ask something the documents do not cover: "What is the name of Pellmore Dental Group's finance director?". It says it cannot find it instead of guessing.

## 3. Summarise a call (2 min)

1. Choose **Company Brain: summarise a document** and start a new chat for Pellmore Dental Group.
2. Ask: "Summarise the call of 27 August 2026."
3. Click the Word download and open the file.

## 4. Draft a proposal (3 min)

1. Choose **Company Brain: draft a proposal** and start a new chat for Pellmore Dental Group.
2. Ask: "Draft a proposal for Pellmore Dental Group."
3. Show the methodology structure: objectives, measures of success, value, then three options with fixed fees paid in monthly instalments in advance, and the optional retainer.
4. Show the "Suggestions, not agreed" box and the "Questions for you" list. Explain that nothing unsourced is presented as fact.
5. Click the PDF download.

## 5. Safety and upkeep (2 min)

1. Double-click **Check health**: services, firewall, last document update, last backup.
2. Explain the nightly encrypted backup and that only the recovery key can open it.
3. Double-click **Show test results** and show the results page.
4. Finish with **Stop AI**.
